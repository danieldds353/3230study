## Answer in two lines

For a definition or comparison: one essential point per line.

For a scenario:

1. Name the problem and relevant concept.
2. Explain the consequence or a justified improvement.

Example:

The user sees the status but misinterprets it, increasing the gulf of evaluation.
Use clear completion wording and the relevant result details.

Equivalent accurate wording matters more than matching the model word for word. Keep every point asked for; shortness alone is not correctness.

## A useful revision loop

1. Start with an unseen round in one week.
2. Recall before checking; explain why the MCQ answer fits.
3. Write short answers before revealing their models.
4. Self-mark and check the two key points for reading-linked prompts.
5. Revisit misses later through Needs review.
6. Complete 8–10 questions in a 60-minute set with answers hidden.

Use the application filter to practise new situations. Use reading-only practice when a concept remains unclear, and slides-only practice to focus on the instructor’s material.
